# Week 13-14 Evidence V2

## Topic
Systems of systems, testing strategy, verification and validation, and coverage.

## Final V2 Submission
- I treated the portfolio as a system of connected parts: page, folders, links, evidence files, and reflections.
- I used a manual verification checklist to test whether the submission package is review-ready.

## Verification Checklist
- Open the main page
- Open each week folder from the page
- Confirm that every week has `evidence-v2.md`
- Confirm that every week has `reflection.md`
- Confirm that the folder names are descriptive
- Confirm that the final project folder is reserved for final packaging

## Coverage Interpretation
- Coverage for this portfolio means all required weeks are represented.
- Coverage also means each week includes both proof and reflection.
- A missing week or missing reflection means incomplete evidence coverage.

## Validation Goal
The portfolio should match the actual review need: fast access, clear organization, and readable weekly evidence.
