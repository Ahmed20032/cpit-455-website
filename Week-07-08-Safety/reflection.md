# Week 07-08 Reflection

- Safety engineering changed how I think about submission mistakes.
- A weak submission is not always the result of weak knowledge; sometimes it comes from unmanaged hazards.
- The fault tree was useful because it turned one big problem into smaller controllable causes.
- ALARP also gave me a practical rule: reduce avoidable risk as much as reasonably possible before submitting.
- I will reuse this style of thinking for the final project package.
